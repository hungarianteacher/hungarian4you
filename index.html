Know before you go

What the simplified citizenship interview is actually like

It's usually more conversational than people expect. You don't need perfect Hungarian — but you do need the right facts, the right preparation, and the ability to talk naturally about your own file.

Pathway 01

By ancestry

You'll need to speak comfortably about your Hungarian ancestor's life — names, places and dates that match your submitted documents precisely.

Pathway 02

By marriage

The focus shifts toward your life together and your household in Hungarian — still grounded in the same standard of everyday, conversational language.

✓ What you actually need

  • Advanced comprehension of spoken Hungarian
  • Simple, clear answers — not a memorised speech
  • Genuine comfort discussing your ancestry or marriage
  • Enough control to recover naturally from a mistake

✕ Mistakes that hurt applicants

  • Over-memorising, which sounds rehearsed and brittle
  • Guessing at dates, names or places instead of knowing them
  • Arriving under-prepared in spoken Hungarian
  • Thin familiarity with your own submitted file
  • Panicking after a small mistake, instead of staying composed
STEP 1
Know your story

Revisit your specific documents regularly. Fix the dates and the Hungarian pronunciation of places in your memory.

STEP 2
Review your forms

Be able to recognise and spell out loud, in Hungarian, exactly what's written on your application.

STEP 3
Practise out of order

Have someone shuffle the questions, so you're listening for meaning rather than following a memorised sequence.

STEP 4
Learn your safety phrases

A few simple, natural Hungarian phrases to keep the conversation moving smoothly if you get stuck.
