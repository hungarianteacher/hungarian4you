How Hungarian numbers work

Perfectly logical

Unlike English ("eleven, twelve, thirteen…"), Hungarian numbers are completely regular from 11 onwards. Once you know 1–10 and the tens, you can build any number — including hundreds, thousands, and years.

Tens + units, no "and"

Compound numbers are formed by simply joining the ten and the unit: huszon + egy = huszonegy (21). No connecting word, no reversal.

Irregular tens

20 = húsz but compounds use huszon-. 30 = harminc (not "három-tíz"). The tens 20–90 each have their own word, then compounds attach directly.

No plural after numbers

In Hungarian, nouns after numbers stay singular: három ház = three house (not houses). This is the opposite of English.

The formula: For 21–99, take the tens word (in its compound form) and add the units word directly. Example: negyven + négy = negyvennégy (44). No space, no hyphen, no extra word. The exact same stacking rule keeps working all the way up: hundreds, thousands, and years are just longer chains of the same building blocks.

Hundreds — 100 to 2000

Hundreds follow the exact same logic as tens: unit + száz. "Egy" (one) is dropped — it's simply száz, never "egyszáz". Just like kettő shortens to két before a noun, 2 shortens the same way here: kétszáz, not "kettőszáz". Past 1000, "ezer" takes over the same way "száz" did: ezerötszáz (1500), kétezer (2000). Combining hundreds with tens and units: keep stacking, with no spaces or joining words — száztíz (110), százhuszonegy (121), háromszázötvennyolc (358), kilencszázkilencvenkilenc (999). The very same rule that built 11–99 now builds 100–1999.

Years, up to 2030

Years are just numbers with ezer (thousand) added the same way száz was added for hundreds: ezer = 1000, kétezer = 2000. Every 20th-century year starts with ezerkilencszáz- ("one-thousand-nine-hundred…") — which is exactly why grandparents' and great-grandparents' birth years, usually somewhere in 1900–1980, nearly always begin the same way. Click any year below to hear it.

Saying "born in [year]": Hungarian adds -ban or -ben to the end of the year. Which one depends on the last word's vowel (a rule called vowel harmony) — e.g. ezerkilencszázharminckettőben született ("was born in 1932").
